How it behaves
  • Breadcrumb = your path. Dinner › Beverages › Hot Drinks. Click any crumb to jump straight there.
  • Folder row (has subcategories) → clicking drills in and extends the breadcrumb.
  • Leaf row (has items) → clicking selects it and its items fill the right column — no more stacked chip rows.
  • Only ever one level of categories on screen, so depth never clutters the panel. ← Up goes one level.
